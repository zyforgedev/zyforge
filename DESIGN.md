# Zyforge design direction

Source direction: the owner's existing black, charcoal and orange anvil-and-flame
logo, Zyforge branding, and requested modern Dieter Rams influence. The website
should make services, useful products and honest project context easy to read.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1. Large direct headings provide the focal point;
service comparisons, an ordered process, project imagery and product rows use
different compositions because they answer different questions. Orange identifies
the next action and selected form state. Black and charcoal continue the existing
brand. Thin boundaries group related content without floating every section.

Existing Outfit body text supports readable long descriptions. Existing Syne
headings retain the brand's geometric character. The supplied logo is used
unchanged. Product facts come from the published offers, and the four projects
are labelled original design studies rather than paid client work.

Business sections are static. Form transitions show a change of step; the CSS and
MotionConfig respect reduced-motion preferences. The concept demos retain their
own original styling, with a persistent disclosure and disabled illustrative
business actions. They do not claim checkout, bookings or third-party integration.

Mobile navigation keeps six readable text links in a horizontally scrollable
header rather than shrinking their labels. The page itself must not overflow.
Form actions stack below 480px, and attachment removal has a 44px target.
